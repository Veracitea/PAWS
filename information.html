01 · Dataset lifecycle

Summary of data pipeline built by the research team.

Please refer to the paper for detailed information. Latest Version of PAWS Database is managed by Tiviatis Sim.

  1. 01
    Select & review policies Woon Jia Hui, Zheng Huanhuan

    Candidate financial and economic policy episodes are checked for dates, scope, measurable relevance, and supporting documentation.

  2. 02
    Collect dated context Tiviatis Sim, Xinming Gao

    Date-bounded queries gather policy-window news while market tables provide contemporaneous numerical context.

  3. 03
    Extract stakeholder actions Tiviatis Sim

    Actors, actions, dates, source links, locations, and structured event-frame fields are extracted from canonical source groups.

  4. 04
    Normalize & coalesce Tiviatis Sim

    Entity aliases are resolved to organizations and related daily actions are merged without losing linked policy, news, or raw-action IDs.

  5. 05
    Validate & release Tiviatis Sim, Xinming Gao, Woon Jia Hui

    AI-assisted checks and human review produce a replay-ready policy–agent–day view with auditable provenance.

02 · Responsible use

Built for research,
not financial advice.

PAWS is a curated replay dataset, not a causal estimate of policy impact or a live trading signal.

Scope

Historical policy environments

The dataset supports research into stakeholder response, action timing, influence, and replay around documented events.

Known limitations

Uneven evidence density

Older and lower-profile policies may have sparser news coverage. Entity resolution and assisted extraction can carry residual noise.

Interpretation

Context, not causality

Market context is aligned in time but does not, on its own, establish that a policy or stakeholder action caused an observed return.

Release practice

Provenance before scale

The public subset should expose construction notes while respecting source rights and excluding material that cannot be redistributed.
